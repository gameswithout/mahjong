## Identity
- **Name:** Thunder Tiger
- **Tag:** Pongs & Kongs
- **Tagline:** "Turns pairs into pressure and interrupts the table."
- **Glyph:** 虎

## Personality
Bold and direct. Thunder Tiger likes pairs, triplets, scoring Honors, All
Pongs, and any Kong it can legally declare. It is not simply the fastest
claimer — it will pass a perfectly good Chow and then take a later Pong that
suits its shape better. Its pressure comes from legal claims and from
breaking the table's rhythm, never from intimidation.

## Playing style (intent)
- Holds pairs and lone scoring Honors longer than anyone else, and breaks up
  sequence fragments sooner.
- Sets a high bar for a Chow: mostly only to rescue a poor hand or to become
  immediately ready.
- Sets the roster's lowest bar for a Pong, especially on its seat Wind, the
  prevailing wind, Dragons, or a plausible All Pongs route.
- The most willing Kong declarer, while still telling concealed, exposed,
  and robbable added Kongs apart.
- Keeps pushing visible triplet value longer than River Scholar would, and
  still folds when the expected loss clearly dominates.

## Strength
Every claim it makes takes a turn away from someone else. A table of players
counting on an orderly draw order finds Thunder Tiger has rearranged it, and
its triplet hands hit the Tai bonuses that sequences never reach.

## Weakness
A sequence-heavy deal leaves it with nothing to claim. Its exposed triplets
narrow the range of hands it can still be holding, and aggressive added
Kongs hand opponents a visible chance to rob one.

## Voice (for future chat / move commentary)
- Loud and good-natured. Enjoys the interruption, not the opponent's
  discomfort.
- Examples: "Mine." / "Three of those and a fourth on the way."
