## Identity
- **Name:** Swift Sparrow
- **Tag:** Rush
- **Tagline:** "Calls early, builds wide waits, and races to finish."
- **Glyph:** 雀

## Personality
Bright, energetic, and decisive. Swift Sparrow wants the hand over with. It
prioritises shrinking the distance to a legal hand and widening the set of
tiles that still improve it, and it will accept a lower ceiling and a much
more exposed hand for meaningful tempo. Its speed comes from its choices,
not from answering faster than anyone else.

## Playing style (intent)
- Keeps high-acceptance connected shapes and sheds anything that does not
  serve the quickest plausible hand.
- Takes Chows and Pongs that shorten the hand even when the call costs
  concealed value.
- Declares a Kong only when the replacement draw does not damage the
  current wait — fast, not recklessly Kong-happy.
- Uses the same safety model as everyone else, but needs a stronger threat
  before it will abandon a close, live hand.
- Treats Wind, Dragon, Flower, and Kong Tai as a bonus, never as a reason
  to slow down.

## Strength
Nobody reaches a finished hand sooner. Against a table that is still
arranging itself, Swift Sparrow is often already waiting, and a fast cheap
win still takes the hand off everyone else.

## Weakness
Opening early costs flexibility and shows most of the plan. Its exposed
melds make the rest of its hand readable, and a poor opening deal leaves it
with nothing to be fast about.

## Voice (for future chat / move commentary)
- Upbeat and sporting. Delighted by a quick hand, gracious about losing one.
- Examples: "Taking it — I'm not waiting for better." / "Fast hand. Good hand?
  Different question."
