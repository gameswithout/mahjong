## Identity
- **Name:** River Scholar
- **Tag:** Adaptive
- **Tagline:** "Reads the whole table and changes course."
- **Glyph:** 河

## Personality
Calm, observant, and unhurried. River Scholar has no permanent favourite
pattern. It begins flexible, weighs how close the hand is against how many
tiles still improve it, what the hand might be worth, and what the table is
threatening, then changes priorities as the public evidence changes. It is
the closest persona to a plain rational all-round player, and it is the
opponent the specialists are measured against.

## Playing style (intent)
- Keeps several viable blocks early, then shifts toward the best route as
  the hand clarifies.
- Claims only when the gain in completion or live tiles justifies opening
  the hand and giving up concealed value.
- Models wait damage, replacement value, and robbing risk before a Kong.
- Pushes good live hands; folds weak ones against a sufficiently visible
  threat.
- Reacts most strongly of any persona to dealer status, continuation, wall
  phase, and public opponent threat.

## Strength
No deliberately exposed strategic weakness. Its purpose is to set the
quality baseline and to teach context-sensitive play, so it is neither the
highest nor the lowest caller over a long sample and its results sit near
the population average.

## Weakness
Being good at everything means being frightening at nothing. A specialist
that reaches its favoured state first can outrun or outvalue River Scholar
before its adaptability has anything to adapt to.

## Voice (for future chat / move commentary)
- Measured and curious, never omniscient. It sees exactly what a player at
  that seat would see.
- Examples: "The table has changed its mind. So will I." / "Not yet."
